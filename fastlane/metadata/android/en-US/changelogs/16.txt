v2.4

* New: Auto-hide settings (IMD+). Pick the apps you want protected and IMD hides your settings by itself when one of them opens, then reopens the app so it starts with nothing to object to. A notification puts it all back. For advanced users.
* IMD's own accessibility service is now hidden with every hide, and restored on the revert.
* IMD+ will not start while a revert is outstanding.
* Dialogs no longer stretch on tablets.
* New Revert to default icon.
