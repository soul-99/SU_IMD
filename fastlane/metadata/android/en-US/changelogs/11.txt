v1.6.6

* The default Settings to unhide on Revert is now Accessibility services only. Everything else in that list is a debugging setting, and switching those back on automatically is a security risk. Existing installs are reset once and told.
* The services manager has an information button explaining what it does and does not control.
* Settings to hide warns about Shizuku's watchdog service, which can turn ADB back on behind you.
