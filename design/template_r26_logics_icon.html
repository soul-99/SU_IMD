r26 — the Logics card icon

An original drawing in the style of the icon you pasted into the screenshot. Nothing is built yet — this is the template and one decision.

Why I have not simply used the Flaticon file. Flaticon's free licence requires visible attribution wherever the icon appears and does not permit redistributing the asset itself — and IMD ships under GPL-3.0, which requires that everything in the source be redistributable on GPL terms. Dropping that PNG into the repo puts the two licences in direct conflict, and it is your name on the project, so it is not a call I will make quietly. What is below is drawn from scratch: same idea — a bulb wired into logic — same flat multi-colour treatment, none of their geometry. If you would rather have their exact icon, buy the Flaticon licence that allows redistribution and send me the file and I will drop it in; that is a real option, just a paid one.

The drawing

A dashed circuit boundary standing for the logic IMD applies, three nodes on it in the app's own greens and teals, a bulb at the centre for the idea, a gear for the mechanism, and the two outcomes IMD actually produces — a green tick for hidden, a red cross for restored. Drawn as an Android VectorDrawable, so it is a few hundred bytes and stays crisp at any density.

The one decision

The nodes and the gear read differently in the two themes above — light uses the app's own dark greens and teals, dark uses the light ones, so the icon belongs to the page it is on. The alternative is one fixed drawing in both, like the shell block you just asked to pin. Both are drawn above; say which and I will build it as a single VectorDrawable (fixed) or as two (theme-aware). The bulb, tick and cross stay the same colours either way — those are meanings, not decoration.
