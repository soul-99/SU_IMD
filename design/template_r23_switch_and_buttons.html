r23 — the switch and the manager's buttons

Static green at ×1.6 (dynamic theme off), drawn at IMD's real geometry: 48×22 dp track, 30 dp thumb, 2 dp rim. Shipped in the r23 zip — tell me if you want a different variant and it is a one-line change.

1 · The checked switch shipped

You said the circle is very hard to see in dark mode. r22 used Material's own pairing — bright primary track, dark onPrimary thumb — which is contrasty but puts the dark half in the knob. Since this switch's thumb overhangs its track, a dark knob reads as a hole punched through a bright pill. r23 swaps them: the track becomes the recess, the thumb becomes the bright thing, and the rim now runs in both states so the two can never merge the way they did on the S22 Ultra in r21.

2 · The two button rows you annotated shipped

Drawn on a translucent card over a photo, which is the condition that made them vanish — a subtle fill has nothing solid behind it. All off / All on stays neutral (your r2b3d pick: it belongs to the switches, not to the filled pair below) but climbs to the top of the surface ladder and gains a 1 dp outline rim, which reads at any card opacity. Hide settings / Revert to default go from secondaryContainer — a muted olive in dark — up to primaryContainer, the strongest container the scheme has.

3 · What changed about the blur itself

Not visual to template, but it is the substance of the round: r19 and r22 both used FLAG_BLUR_BEHIND, which frosts everything behind the window on the same display-wide layer that does dim-behind — it is not bounded by the window at any size, which is why r22's window-shrink changed nothing and your home screen is still blurred edge to edge. r23 uses Window.setBackgroundBlurRadius instead, the API that blurs only the window's own area, clipped to its rounded background. The page behind a frosted manager is now identical to the page behind any other dialog.

The blur dialog also now prints whether your device will frost a window at all, so the S22 Ultra question answers itself on the device rather than from my guess.
